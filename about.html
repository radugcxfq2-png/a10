ABOUT / WHO WRITES THIS

Plain answers about wristwatches.

Watcher is a small independent publication for people who own a watch, or are about to, and want to understand it without wading through jargon.

Watch writing tends to assume you already know what a lug is and care deeply about finishing. We start further back. What do the numbers on the caseback mean? Will this fit my wrist? Is my watch running well, or does it need attention? Those questions have clear answers, and we try to give them in as few words as possible.

We publish two guides and three calculators. Each is checked against manufacturers’ published specifications and the relevant international standards for water resistance and accuracy, and reviewed by a second reader before it goes live.

01

No sales

We do not sell watches, straps or parts, and we do not accept payment to feature any brand or model.

02

No valuations

We do not appraise, authenticate or comment on the resale prospects of any watch.

03

No guesswork

Where advice depends on a specific model, we say so and point you to its manual or a qualified watchmaker.

04

Open to correction

Found an error? Tell us through the contact page and we will fix it and note the change.
